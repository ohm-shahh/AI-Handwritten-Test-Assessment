1. Executive Summary

Grading handwritten assignments manually is time-consuming, prone to human fatigue, and often lacks immediate, structured feedback for students. The AI Tutor System automates this workflow from end to end:

An instructor creates an assignment with custom model answers and rubrics. Students submit photographs of their handwritten notebook pages or PDFs. Our backend cleans the images using classical computer vision (OpenCV), converts handwriting to digital text using deep-learning OCR, and evaluates the answer semantically against the ground-truth rubric using a local Large Language Model (Ollama gemma3 / phi3).

4. Key Architectural Innovations

⚡ Smart 3-Tier PDF Fallback:

Digital PDFs are parsed instantaneously (< 0.4s) directly from font glyphs with 100% character accuracy using pdfplumber and PyPDF2, while scanned or handwritten pages automatically route through OpenCV image binarization and neural OCR.

🧠 100% Free & Local Privacy:

All evaluations run locally on your system using Ollama LLMs without sending student answers or exam questions to external paid APIs, ensuring zero recurring cloud costs and complete institutional data privacy.
